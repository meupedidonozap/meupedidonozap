- Prospect registration validation is separate from checkout validation and scoped by the official store slug, so allowing an empty document at registration cannot weaken order submission or other stores.
- Android PDFs use the user-selected document destination without broad storage permission; previews render locally and never write orders. DiColore GPS uses foreground native calls, and contact permission is read-only with no agenda reads/uploads, preserving other stores and browser behavior.
- In the Android APK every WhatsApp link/popup is routed through the native launcher (app scheme, then universal link) by one document-level interceptor, because the WebView cannot hand links to other apps; browsers keep normal links. APK PDF buttons always open the on-screen preview, and saving happens only from it.
- Invoice item lists read the mirrored NF items by the invoice external key and fall back to billed order items only when the invoice has none, through one tested resolver, so the screen shows what was actually invoiced.
- The update button never fails on a slow replacement worker: after a bounded wait it drops only the app-shell precache and reloads from the network, keeping the registration, push subscription, cart and session; release.json may carry a message shown in the mandatory notice and once after updating, only on the released stores.
